THE PROBLEM

Hiring African talent still runs on duct tape.

The talent is ready. The infrastructure to hire, pay, and cover them isn't — so every enterprise rebuilds the same broken pipeline from scratch.

01 — FRICTION

FX eats the offer

Cross-border payroll gets routed through three intermediaries before it clears — if it clears. Talent gets paid late, in the wrong currency, minus fees nobody agreed to.

02 — RISK

Compliance is manual

Contracts, KYC, and local tax rules get handled ad hoc per hire. One missed step and the enterprise — not the hub — carries the liability.

03 — FRAGMENTATION

No single system of record

Discovery lives in one spreadsheet, contracts in another tool, payroll with a third vendor, and insurance — usually — nowhere at all.

THE INFRASTRUCTURE

Four endpoints. One continuous rail.

Discovery, hiring, payroll, and insurance aren't separate tools bolted together — they're one system, built so each step hands off cleanly to the next.

01

Discover

Browse vetted talent pools — scoped to a specific hub, or open across the full network — filtered by skill, seniority, and availability.

GET/v1/talents/discover
02

Hire

Propose terms, currency, and coverage. The talent accepts through a single public link — no account required — and a real contract is generated instantly.

POST/v1/engagements/:id/contract
03

Pay

Real money, not a simulation. Disburse on schedule, in the currency that actually clears, with full ledger and payout history on every contract.

POST/v1/payroll/disburse
04

Insure

Health coverage tied directly to the contract from day one — not an afterthought bolted on once the enterprise gets around to it.

GET/v1/coverage_status
